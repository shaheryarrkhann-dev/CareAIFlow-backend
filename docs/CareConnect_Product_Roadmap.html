Growth Opportunities
Four Features to Build Next

These four features represent the highest-value additions to the platform. Each addresses a real, daily pain point for AFH operators and their staff.

1

Discharge & Transfer Workflow

Most immediate clinical gap — operators will ask for this soon

Residents move through different stages of care — some are discharged, some transfer to another facility, and some require hospital stays. The next step for the platform is a complete discharge and transfer workflow that handles every scenario cleanly and keeps the full resident history intact.

  • Discharge a resident with reason, date, and destination
  • Transfer a resident between facilities within the same organization
  • Maintain full history — the resident record is preserved, not deleted
  • Auto-close open medications, care plans, and tasks on discharge
Clinical Compliance
2

Family / Guardian Portal

Emotional value — families pay for peace of mind

Families want to stay connected to their loved one's care without having to call the facility every time. A dedicated family portal gives them real-time visibility into daily care — reducing unnecessary calls, building trust, and setting this facility apart from others that offer no family transparency at all.

  • View daily progress notes, medication compliance, and behavioral status
  • Receive push notifications for incidents, missed medications, or health changes
  • Message staff directly through the platform
  • Sign consent and admission documents electronically from home
New Revenue Family Engagement

Four Features to Build Next
3

Mobile App

Caregivers are on the floor — not at a desk

Every workflow in the current platform assumes the user is sitting at a computer. But caregivers give medications at 7am, write incident notes in the hallway, and check vitals at the bedside. A mobile app brings the platform to where the work actually happens — reducing paper notes and improving documentation accuracy.

  • Record a MAR dose directly at the medication cart with one tap
  • Capture incident photos on your phone — attached directly to the report
  • Voice-to-text progress notes while walking the floor
  • Push notifications 10 minutes before a medication is due
  • Works on both iOS and Android (React Native)
Caregiver Experience Documentation Quality
4

Staff Scheduling Module

Operators are managing shifts in WhatsApp right now

Washington State requires a minimum caregiver-to-resident ratio at all times. Despite this, scheduling is the one critical operation still handled completely outside the platform — in WhatsApp groups, paper sheets, or spreadsheets. When someone calls in sick, operators scramble manually. Scheduling inside the platform creates both compliance coverage and long-term stickiness.

  • Weekly shift scheduling published to all staff
  • Staff can view their assigned shifts and request time off
  • Automatic alert when a shift is uncovered and minimum ratios are at risk
  • Know exactly who was on duty at the time of any incident
  • Once scheduling lives here, the operator will never leave the platform
Operations Compliance Retention
Execution Plan
Recommended Roadmap

A phased approach — fix what's broken first, then build new features in order of client impact. Each phase delivers visible value before moving to the next.

Phase 1 — Short Term
Discharge & Transfer Workflow
  • Add discharge fields to Resident schema (date, reason, type)
  • Build discharge and transfer API endpoints
  • Build discharge modal and transfer flow in frontend
  • Auto-close open medications, care plans, tasks on discharge
Phase 2 — Medium Term
Family / Guardian Portal
  • Design and build dedicated guardian-facing UI
  • Controlled read access to notes, medications, behavioral trends
  • Incident and health change push notifications to family
  • In-app messaging between family and staff
  • Remote document signing
Phase 3 — Medium Term
Mobile App (React Native)
  • MAR dose recording on mobile
  • Photo capture for incidents
  • Voice-to-text progress notes
  • Medication due and missed dose push notifications
  • iOS and Android deployment
Phase 4 — Long Term
Staff Scheduling Module
  • Weekly shift scheduling and staff availability management
  • Minimum caregiver ratio compliance alerts
  • Shift coverage notifications
  • On-duty staff linked to incident and MAR records
